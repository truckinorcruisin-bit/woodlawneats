Sunday cook flow

Which appliance is running when. Bars that overlap are meant to overlap — set your dinner time and work backward.

Grocery list

Everything for all three dinners. Quantities feed two adults and two elementary-age kids, with enough slack for one packed lunch.

What's in the fridge when you're done

Cool everything uncovered for 20 minutes before it goes in containers, or you'll steam it soggy.

The three dinners

Sunday is the cook. Nights two and three are assembly — 25 minutes or less, start to plate.

Ground rules

The three base recipes below replace the store-bought products that would otherwise sneak onion powder or wheat into almost every plan. Make them once a month and freeze.

Label landmines

These almost always contain onion or garlic powder even when the front of the package says nothing:

  • Boxed and canned broth or stock — including "low sodium" and "organic"
  • Taco, fajita, chili, and BBQ seasoning packets
  • Jarred salsa, marinara, enchilada sauce, curry paste
  • Rotisserie chicken and pre-marinated meats
  • Worcestershire, most steak sauces, many mustards
  • Bouillon cubes, "natural flavors" on snack foods
  • Soy sauce (wheat) — use tamari and check it's certified GF

Base 1 · Allium-free chicken broth

Makes about 3 quarts. Freezes flat in quart bags for four months.

  • 2 chicken carcasses or 3 lb wings, 4 carrots, 4 celery ribs, 1 fennel bulb quartered
  • 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp black peppercorns, 1 tsp fennel seed, a big handful of parsley stems
  • Cover with 4 qt cold water. Bare simmer 3 hours, never boiling. Strain, salt to taste.
  • Fennel and celery do the aromatic work that onion normally does. Do not skip the fennel.

Base 2 · All-purpose rub

Goes on everything that touches the grill, smoker, or air fryer. Makes about 1 cup.

  • 4 Tbsp smoked paprika, 3 Tbsp kosher salt, 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 Tbsp each ground cumin, ground coriander, black pepper
  • 2 tsp ground fennel, 1 tsp celery seed, 1 tsp dry mustard
  • Optional heat: 1–2 tsp chipotle powder. Keeps six months in a jar.

Base 3 · BBQ and tomato sauce

One tomato base, two finishes. Both freeze well.

  • BBQ: 1 can tomato paste, 1 cup water, ⅓ cup cider vinegar, ⅓ cup molasses, 2 Tbsp Dijon, 2 Tbsp rub above. Simmer 20 min.
  • Marinara: 2 Tbsp olive oil, 1 diced fennel bulb, 1 grated carrot, sweat 10 min. Add 28 oz crushed tomatoes, 1 tsp oregano, pinch chili. Simmer 30 min.

Flavor without alliums

  • Sweet base: fennel bulb, celery, carrot, roasted red pepper
  • Sharp: ginger, Dijon, capers, olives, horseradish, radish
  • Savory depth: tomato paste, chickpea miso, GF fish sauce, dried mushroom powder, anchovy
  • Bright: lemon and lime zest, vinegar, fresh herbs added off the heat
  • Warm: smoked paprika, cumin, coriander, fennel seed, celery seed

Two questions worth settling

  • Garlic-infused oil. If the issue is FODMAPs, infused oil is usually fine — the fructans don't dissolve into oil. If it's a true allium sensitivity, it's out. Nothing in these plans depends on it.
  • Pork. Treated as red meat here, so it's absent. If pork tenderloin is fair game, it swaps one-for-one anywhere chicken thighs appear on the grill or smoker.
  • Cross-contact. If gluten is celiac rather than preference, run GF items on their own grill grate and use a separate air fryer basket liner.
